\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

Published full-resolution CRA5 trains on 1979--2021 and tests on
2022--2023~\cite{han2024}. Thus 2018 is held out for our corrector but overlaps
CRA5 training. Dates are publication-reported, not verified from a checkpoint
training log. Frozen full-year 2022 tests one unseen year, not robustness across
climate regimes. One region, neural codec and few corrector architectures limit
generalisation.

Mean-rate matching compares global/regional tasks, not identical domains.
Per-frame rate coefficients of variation are 3.70\% for MPCA-only and 0.95\%
for CRA5. Payload excludes fixed bases and correction weights; correction adds
inference cost. Derived \texttt{tcwv} partly measures derivation error.
No pointwise or per-block improvement is guaranteed.

Bootstrap intervals condition on fitted checkpoints and year; seven-day blocks
do not establish independence beyond that lag. Spatial cells are correlated.
Roughness covaries with elevation/geography; western lowland bins contain only
29--31 cells. Below-ground values complicate mountain comparisons.
Both terrain analyses describe associations, not causality. Matched dilation
expands theoretical support but retains concentrated gradients; one seed and
dilation pattern cannot exclude broader context or optimisation effects.
Unequal group sizes and error magnitudes affect the shared residual-MSE loss.
