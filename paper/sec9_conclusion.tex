\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

A frozen U-Net reduces direct19 CRA5 nRMSE by 25.8\% in 2018 and 26.4\% in
unseen 2022, without extra frame bits or fine-tuning. Near/upper gains are
32--33\%/3.5--3.6\%. Matched bottleneck dilation retains a roughly eightfold
split, concentrated gradient energy and slightly worse overall error.
Actual roughness associates with larger western-land errors and gains;
proxy-elevation controls retain the negative 3.23-to-1.14 ratio result.
Model-relative predictability transfers to one unseen year; terrain causality,
architecture and broader robustness remain open. Shared weights and computation
are additional costs.
