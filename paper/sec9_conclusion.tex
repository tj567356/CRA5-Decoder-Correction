\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

A frozen U-Net reduces direct19 CRA5 nRMSE by 25.8\% in 2018 and 26.4\% in
unseen 2022, without extra frame bits or fine-tuning. Near/upper gains are
32--33\%/3.5--3.6\%. Matched bottleneck dilation retains a roughly eightfold
split, concentrated gradient energy and slightly worse overall error.
On western land, ERA5-orography roughness is associated with larger baseline
errors and correction gains; corrected errors remain higher on rough ground. Elevation-stratified checks do not establish a causal mechanism.
Model-relative predictability persists in one year outside CRA5's reported
training period; terrain causality, architecture and broader robustness remain
open. Shared weights and computation are additional costs.
